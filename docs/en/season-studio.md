# Season Editor (Season Studio)

**Power Pros Wii 2008 Season Studio** sets up Season mode and MLB Life seasons: which year they start in, with which schedule, DH and leagues. It opens from the launcher: **SETTINGS → TOOLS → SEASON EDITOR**. It is `SeasonStudio.exe`, next to the game.

It reads and changes no game files: it only writes in a mod's folder (`mod.toml` and `schedule/<year>.csv`) and switches mods on or off in `Config.toml`, as the launcher's MODS screen does.

## The window

- **Left, NEXT NEW GAME**: how a new game will start with the enabled mods and the ENHANCEMENTS options:
  - the year,
  - which year the roster is from (the ages),
  - which years have a real schedule and which get one generated by the game,
  - the DH and the leagues.

  Each one says where it comes from: a mod or ENHANCEMENTS. When two enabled mods ask for different things, a warning shows which one is used (the first by name, as the game does).
- **Top, Mod**: the mod being set up, whether it is switched on in the game (with **Switch on** / **Switch off**), and **+ New mod** to create one, already switched on.
- **SETTINGS**: the mod's settings, each with its explanation below.
  - **Start year** (`start_year`): 2008 is the game's.
  - **Roster year (ages)** (`roster_year`): which year the roster is from, so ages come out right. The usual choice is «Same as the start».
  - **DH**: «As in ENHANCEMENTS», «In both leagues» (`dh = "both"`) or «Original (American League)» (`dh = "original"`).
  - **Leagues**: «As in ENHANCEMENTS», «15 and 15» (`[divisions]`, Houston in the AL West) or «Original (14 and 16)» (`leagues = "original"`). A mod with other divisions in its `mod.toml` shows «Its own».
  - **Real schedules**: choose **From** and **To** (2002 to 2026; 2020 is missing because it had 60 games) and press **Download**. It gets the major leagues' schedules (statsapi.mlb.com) and saves them in the mod. If the mod had no start year, it gets the first one. The mod's years are listed below: press one to see it.
- **SCHEDULE**: one of the mod's years, month by month.
  - With a team chosen, each day shows its opponent («vs NYY» at home, «@ SF» away).
  - With «All», how many games that day has.
  - Press a day to see its games with their time (US Eastern).

  The dates are the ones the game plays: a season that started before March 19 or ended after October 1 is moved a few days, and games on a day with more than 16 move to a nearby day. The note above says so.

Changes apply to the next new game. Games already started stay as they were. If the game is open, close it and open it again.

## Testing without a window

- `SeasonStudio.exe --selftest`: self-test.
- `SeasonStudio.exe --download 2025 2026 --mod <folder>`: the download without a window.
- On opening: `--mod <folder> --tab calendar --year 2025 --team NYY --month 4 --day 5 --lang es|en`.

The code is in `tools/season-studio`. It shares the look and the `Config.toml` reading with the Texture Studio, and the season rules with the runtime (`season_pack.h`, `season_text.h`, `season_schedule.h`, `season_schedule_mlb.h`).
